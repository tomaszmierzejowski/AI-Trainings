## What I'm Honest About

- **ROI numbers:** Were never formally calculated for most projects.
- **"100% success rate":** Was a buzzword, not a metric. It appeared in marketing materials and has been permanently banned.
- **Performance improvements:** Were "felt" not benchmarked in most cases. The Proof of Speed app startup improvement was perceived, not measured.
- **Migration side effects:** Some bugs were introduced during the internal Angular-React migration. Some bugs were also fixed. Neither outcome was unexpected.
- **Discovery scope:** The 87% cost savings and speed improvements apply to the discovery/analysis phase only. They do not represent savings on the full modernization program.
