## Software Project Success Rates

### Standish Group CHAOS Report
<!-- [Source: VFR §5] -->

**Correct phrasing:** "31% of IT projects succeed outright."

**Citation:** Standish Group, CHAOS 2020 Report.

**Important caveats:**
- Do NOT cite as "2020-2024" or any range — the 31% figure is from the 2020 report specifically.
- Do NOT say "73% of modernization projects fail" — that number does not exist in any Standish report. The 31% figure covers all IT projects, not modernization specifically.
- The Standish CHAOS methodology has been criticized: see Eveleens & Verhoef, "The Rise and Fall of the Chaos Report Figures," IEEE Software, 2010.

### BCG Digital Transformation Success
<!-- [Source: VFR §5] -->

**Correct phrasing:** "35% of digital transformation initiatives succeed, based on 850+ companies surveyed."

**Citation:** BCG, 2021 Global Digital Transformation Survey.

## AI Productivity Research

### METR Study — AI Impact on Experienced Developers
<!-- [Source: VFR §5] -->

**Correct phrasing:** "Experienced developers took 19% longer on complex tasks when using AI coding tools."

**Citation:** Becker et al., arXiv:2507.09089, July 2025 (METR study).

**Important caveats:**
- Scope: 16 experienced developers using early-2025 AI tools
- The metric is +19% completion time (slower), not -19% (faster)
- This measures experienced developers on complex, real-world tasks — not juniors or simple tasks
- Tomasz's interpretation: not a condemnation of AI, but evidence that adoption requires practice and the right workflow (see AI_MINDSET_POSITIONS.md for his three-point defense)

### NAB + Cursor Deployment
<!-- [Source: VFR §5] -->

**Correct phrasing:** "6,000 developers, 3x faster with Cursor."

**Citation:** Cursor blog, April 2026.

**Important caveat:** This is a vendor case study (Cursor's own blog). Cite as such — not as independent research.
